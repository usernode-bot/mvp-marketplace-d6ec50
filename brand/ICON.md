# App icon

The shopping-bag glyph (Lucide `shopping-bag`, 2px round strokes) in white on
a solid `#7C3AED` (brand-600) full-bleed square. No gradient and no shadow:
the purple square and the white line glyph are the whole design, matching the
app's favicon and header logo.

- Source: `icon.svg` (512 × 512, square corners; the platform tile rounds it)
- Raster: `icon.png`, declared in `dapp.json` as `{ "icon": { "image": "brand/icon.png" } }`
- The glyph sits in the middle 288px (Lucide 24-unit grid scaled ×12), so the
  padding stays balanced at every tile size.
